# Changes

This iteration was mainly to focus on UX fixes and improvements.

- **Navigation:** Courses replaces groups. According to feedback about
  finding groups through their courses rather than groups.
- **Resources:** GitLab, Google Drive, group chat and the GitLab issue
  are now placeholder links.
- **Submission:** group page shows deadline and 1 of 3 items ready.
  Te warning shows that two items are not ready/submitted etc.
- **Labels:** Email replaces Nudge and Attached replaces
   Included to match the actions
- **Activity:** No commits or edits for 14 days says what activity
  means in place of an unclear 'No activity'
- **Unassign:** email forms have Cancel buttons. The Unassign
  button returns the task to its original case
- **Emails:** the member email form starts with a subject ENGR101 Group 7 now
- **Contrast:** clearer colour ranking and accessability for card importance

I did not add a built in chat as of now, as my opinion is that it creates another place to check for messages
